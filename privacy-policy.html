Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how ScholarHub collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information while you use our platform.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ScholarHub, an educational platform designed to help students connect with study partners, collaborate in study groups, access learning resources, and improve their academic journey.

We are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring transparency in how your data is handled. By using ScholarHub, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide you with the best learning experience, ScholarHub may collect the following information when you use our platform:

  • Personal Information: Your full name, email address, educational level, school or institution, location (if provided), and profile photo.
  • Account Information: Your username, password (stored securely in an encrypted format), and account preferences.
  • Learning Information: Study interests, selected subjects, study groups, mentorship requests, and academic preferences.
  • Technical Information: Browser type, device information, operating system, IP address, pages visited, and general usage statistics that help improve the platform.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect helps us provide a secure, personalized, and effective learning experience.

  • Create and manage your ScholarHub account.
  • Connect you with study partners and mentors.
  • Recommend relevant study groups and learning resources.
  • Improve platform performance and user experience.
  • Respond to your questions and provide customer support.
  • Send important notifications, security updates, and service announcements.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorized access to the platform.

4. How We Share Your Information

ScholarHub values your privacy and does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share limited information only in the following situations:

  • When required by applicable laws or legal authorities.
  • To protect the safety, rights, and security of our users and platform.
  • With trusted service providers that help us operate ScholarHub, subject to strict confidentiality obligations.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

While we strive to keep your information secure, no method of transmitting data over the internet or storing data electronically is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Choices

As a ScholarHub user, you have the right to:

  • Access and update your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Request the deletion of your account, subject to applicable requirements.
  • Choose whether to upload optional profile information such as your profile picture or location.

7. Cookies

ScholarHub may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, improve functionality, and enhance your browsing experience.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, although doing so may affect certain features of the platform.

8. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites or services for educational purposes.

ScholarHub is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of those external websites. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any third-party services they visit.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements, legal requirements, or new platform features.

Any updates will be posted on this page together with the revised "Last Updated" date.

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
